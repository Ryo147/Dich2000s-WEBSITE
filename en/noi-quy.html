Review Rules

Last updated: 05/11/2026. Applies to display names and comments in the reviews section for Vietnamese localizations.

This English version is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Vietnamese version, the Vietnamese version prevails. Read the Vietnamese version

1. General principles

Reviews are for players to share real experiences with a Vietnamese localization. Both praise and criticism are welcome, including low scores: the group does not remove a review merely because it is negative or because the star rating is low.

2. Content that will be removed

  • Insulting, abusing, threatening or discriminating against individuals or groups of people.
  • Disclosing other people's personal information (real name, address, phone number, private social media accounts).
  • Spam, advertising, links, or mass-repeated content.
  • Impersonating administrators or group members, including choosing a name similar to theirs.
  • Content that violates Vietnamese law, or calls for violating the law.

3. How violations are handled

Only the violating text is removed, and it is replaced with the line "Removed for violating the rules" ("Đã gỡ vì vi phạm nội quy"). The star rating is still kept in the average score. If you edit your comment to comply with the rules, an administrator may review it and restore it.

The administrators are the reserved accounts NhuaInox, nhantc and megabomb. Names identical or similar to these cannot be registered, to prevent impersonation.

4. Reporting and contact

Each comment has a "Report" button. You can also send an email to dich2000s.vn.localizer@gmail.com with the game name, the poster's name and the reason. The group reviews reports as capacity allows and does not commit to a specific processing time; when a competent state authority requests removal of content, the group will act in accordance with that request.

5. Your data

Your name, star rating and comment are displayed publicly. See how the website stores and deletes data in the Privacy Policy. You can delete your account and all reviews yourself with the "Delete my account and reviews" button at the bottom of the reviews section.
