1. Information you provide when reviewing a Vietnamese localization

You can browse the website without providing any information. Only when you want to review a Vietnamese localization does the website collect: (a) the display name you choose, (b) a star rating from 1 to 5, (c) a comment (optional), and (d) the time of submission. The website does not collect your real name, email, phone number or address. Please do not put your own or anyone else's real personal information in the name or the comment.

Public: your display name, star rating and comment are shown publicly to everyone, including on the home page (selected at random). You agree to this by ticking the consent box before submitting.

Account: your first review automatically creates an account under the name you chose and issues a 3-word code so you can log in again. The code is shown only once; the group stores only a hash of the code and cannot view it or reissue it to you. Your login session uses one strictly necessary technical cookie (d2k_sess, httpOnly, expires automatically after 21 days of inactivity, maximum 2 devices); this cookie is not an analytics cookie and does not depend on your choice in the cookie banner. To prevent abuse, the website temporarily stores a hash of your IP address (not the original IP) to limit the number of actions, for at most 24 hours.

Moderation: administrators may remove the text of a comment that violates the rules (insults, discrimination, spam, links, other people's personal information); the removed text is replaced with "Removed for violating the rules" ("Đã gỡ vì vi phạm nội quy"). The star rating is still kept in the average score because the group does not remove a review merely because its score is low. Each comment has a "Report" button.

Email (optional): the website does not collect email when you review or create an account. Only if you enter it yourself on the Account page does the group store the email address, after you click the verification link (the link expires after 1 hour; an unverified email is not saved to the account). The email is used only to send security notices for the account, such as when a request to change the 3-word code is made, and is not used for advertising or shown publicly. Emails are sent through Gmail (Google). You can remove your email at any time on the Account page; deleting your account also deletes the email.

Retention: accounts and reviews are kept until you delete your account yourself (section 4). The website does not currently delete inactive accounts automatically. Anti-abuse data (IP hashes, attempt counters) expires automatically within at most 24 hours; comment-report counters are kept for at most 30 days.

Children: the website is not directed at children under 16. If you are under 16, please ask your parent or guardian to consent before posting your name and comment publicly.

Detailed rules: Review Rules.

The website does not run ads and the group does not sell your data.

2. Data collected to count views and people currently online

To display "online" and "views" in the footer, the website processes the following data:

  • Random visitor ID (key d2k_vid in the browser's localStorage). This is a random string that contains no information about you. The ID is sent to the server to tell whether a person is currently viewing the site and to avoid double-counting views within 24 hours.
  • IP address. The website does not store the original IP. The IP is one-way hashed (SHA-256 with a salt) and then used to prevent spam: limiting the number of calls within 60 seconds and not double-counting the same page within 10 minutes. This hash is deleted automatically after the periods above.
  • Online status: the random visitor ID and the time of last activity. A person is considered offline after 75 seconds of inactivity, and the record is cleaned up when the server recalculates the number of people online.
  • View counters: only aggregate totals (site-wide and per project), not tied to any individual.

The visitor ID and the hashed IP address above are created and sent to the server only after you click "Accept" in the cookie banner. If you decline or have not chosen, the website does not create a visitor ID and does not send view-count or online-status data (you can still see the aggregate totals). The visitor ID stays in your browser until you decline again or clear the site data.

The data is stored in the Upstash Redis service, running through Vercel. In addition, the website uses Google Analytics (ID G-5D3W8D9JH1) to compile aggregate visit statistics such as pages viewed, device type, region and traffic source. This service is loaded only after you click "Accept" in the cookie banner. If you decline or have not chosen, the website does not call Google Analytics and Google receives no visit data from this site. See also section 3.

3. Cookies and third-party services

The visitor ID in section 2 is in localStorage, not a cookie, and exists only if you accept. Google Analytics specifically, if you accept, will set Google cookies (usually with names starting with _ga) to distinguish visits. If you decline or have not chosen, this cookie is not set.

To display the page, your browser loads resources from third parties, and they will receive your IP address as is normal for how the internet works:

  • Google Analytics / Google Tag Manager (googletagmanager.com): visit statistics, subject to Google's own privacy policy.
  • Vercel: the server that runs the website, which may record access logs.
  • Vercel Speed Insights: measures page load speed so the group can improve the website.
  • GitHub (api.github.com): when you open a page that shows download counts and releases, your browser calls GitHub itself to fetch this list (the result is temporarily stored in localStorage). GitHub receives your IP address.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com): fonts.
  • cdnjs / Cloudflare: the Font Awesome and GSAP libraries.
  • YouTube (youtube-nocookie.com): videos embedded on the Get the Patch page, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube receives your IP address when the page loads, and may set its own cookies only when you click play. The group does not control this.

When you click through to GitHub, Discord or Facebook, those services apply their own policies.

4. Your rights

You can change your cookie choice at any time: . You can also delete the visitor ID at any time by clearing the website's site data in your browser. The next visit will create a new ID that is no longer linked to the old one.

Review data (section 1): once logged in, you open "Account" in the menu to change your display name (once every 7 days), view your reviews, and edit your reviews yourself at any time, and there is a "Delete my account and reviews" button at the bottom of the reviews section to delete the account together with all reviews and comments. If you lose your code and cannot log in, contact the group as described in section 6; the group will need you to prove that you own that name (for example by recalling the content and time of your review) before deleting on your behalf.

As for the data in section 2: because it contains no directly identifying information, the group has no technical means to look up or delete one person's records individually. These records expire automatically according to the time limits in section 2 (60 seconds, 10 minutes, 24 hours, 75 seconds), and the view counters are only aggregate totals, not tied to any individual.

You still have the rights under the Luật Bảo vệ dữ liệu cá nhân 2025 (Law on Personal Data Protection 2025), including the right to be informed, to withdraw consent, to object, and to lodge complaints about your data. The fastest way to exercise them is to change your cookie choice or clear the site data as described above. If you need to ask more, contact the group as described in section 6. The group will respond within 2 working days and state clearly what it can or cannot do with your request.

5. Storage and processing abroad

The data in sections 1 and 2 is processed through the services of Upstash, Vercel, Google (Gmail, if you add an email) and (if you accept analytics cookies) Google Analytics. These services may host their servers outside Vietnam.
