01.

Origins

Already very used to English interfaces, this group of friends found a bigger passion: localizing games into Vietnamese, painting a picture where a global language meets the mother tongue.

02.

Philosophy

Translation isn't about replacing, it's about conveying. Every line and every word deserves careful attention. No cutting corners, no translating just to get it done.

03.

Commitment

Our patches are always free, and we never charge in any form.
